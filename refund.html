GLOBALTOOLS • CUSTOMER POLICY

Refund Policy

Information about digital-product purchases, delivery problems and how to contact support.

1. Digital products

GlobalToolsNow offers digital products such as downloadable Excel workbooks and access to online dashboard features. Each product page identifies the product and price before checkout. Review the product description and supported formats before purchasing.

2. Delivery

After payment is confirmed, the checkout flow provides the applicable delivery or account-access instructions. If a link does not work, access is unavailable or a file is missing, contact support so the issue can be investigated.

3. Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed according to the applicable consumer-protection law and the terms presented at purchase. If a product was not delivered, cannot be accessed or differs materially from its description, explain the issue so it can be reviewed. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory rights provided by applicable law.

4. How to contact us

Use the GlobalTools contact form and choose “Product or download” or “Payment or billing.” Include the product name, the email used at checkout and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords or full payment-card information.

5. Payments

Online payments are processed through Stripe checkout. GlobalToolsNow does not ask customers to send card numbers or banking credentials by email or support form.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated when products, checkout flows or applicable requirements change.
