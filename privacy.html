Privacy Policy

This policy describes information handled when you visit GlobalToolsNow, use its tools, submit a support request or interact with advertising and analytics services.

1. Website covered by this policy

This policy applies to GlobalToolsNow.com and the online tools and digital products available through this website. GlobalTools provides general-purpose utilities for documents, PDFs, images, text, calculations, development and related tasks.

2. Information you submit

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic, message and page you identify in the request. The server adds a reference number, timestamp and support status. We use this information to understand and follow up on the request. Support records are stored in the site's configured server data directory and retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support history or meet applicable obligations.

Do not include passwords, full payment-card numbers, authentication tokens, banking credentials or confidential documents in support messages.

3. Data entered into tools

Many tools are designed to process text or files in your browser. When that local processing path is used, the input is handled by the browser rather than uploaded to our application server. Not every tool has identical behavior: some features load third-party libraries, and certain features may send the specific input needed to an external service. For example, QR-code images are generated through an external QR service. Tool pages may describe these limits. Avoid entering sensitive information unless you have checked the tool's processing behavior.

4. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The website uses browser storage to remember preferences such as the selected language. Google Analytics and advertising services may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers or similar technologies to measure use, prevent abuse, support advertising and provide or improve their services. You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some website features.

5. Google Analytics

GlobalToolsNow uses Google Analytics to understand visits and tool interactions and to diagnose or improve the site. Depending on the service settings, information may include pages viewed, events, approximate location derived from technical signals, device and browser details, referral information and interaction timing. Google processes this data under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Google AdSense and advertising

GlobalToolsNow uses Google AdSense code and may display advertisements when the site and account are eligible. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve, limit, measure and personalize ads, subject to applicable settings, consent and policies. Advertising partners may process technical information such as browser information, IP address or interaction data in accordance with their terms.

You can review or change ad-personalization settings at Google Ads Settings. For information about how Google uses information on partner sites, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required for advertising to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site operator must configure a Google-certified consent management platform that meets Google's applicable requirements before serving personalized ads in those regions. Users should use the consent choices provided on the site where available.

7. Service providers and external links

We use service providers to operate the website and may rely on third-party services for analytics, advertising, payment processing, language translation, document libraries or specific tool functions. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Links to external sites are provided for convenience; GlobalToolsNow does not control their privacy practices.

8. Security and data minimization

Reasonable technical and operational measures are used to operate the site and protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please submit only information necessary for the requested task.

9. Children

GlobalToolsNow is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically designed for children. The website does not intentionally ask children to submit personal information for an account or profile.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. Use the contact form to send a privacy request. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request safely.

11. Updates to this policy

This policy may change as the site, tools, services or legal requirements change. The current version is published on this page.
