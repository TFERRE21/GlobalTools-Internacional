Contact

Use this channel to send suggestions, report problems or communicate errors in our tools.

Email

To contact us, email contato@globaltools-international.example.

What should I include in an error report?

Include the tool name, browser used, a description of the problem and, when possible, a generic example that allows us to reproduce the situation.

Privacy and security

Do not send passwords, personal documents, banking information, authentication tokens or other confidential information by email. Prefer fictional examples or non-identifying data when reporting a problem.

Suggestions

We also welcome suggestions for new tools, usability improvements, translations and content corrections.
