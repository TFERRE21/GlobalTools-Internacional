About GlobalTools

Simple, free and accessible digital tools for solving everyday tasks directly in your browser.

What is GlobalTools?

GlobalTools is a platform of online tools designed to make tasks that normally require separate programs or services easier. The catalog includes utilities for PDFs, documents, images, text, development, conversion, calculations, dates, security and other practical needs.

The goal is simple: find a tool, provide or select the required data, process it and get the result without creating an account for core features.

How do the tools work?

Whenever technically possible, processing happens directly in your browser. This is especially useful for file operations because you can work locally without relying on a permanent server upload. Some features use external libraries or services when required to perform the task.

Content and quality

Each interactive tool aims to explain its purpose, how to use it, examples, limitations and frequently asked questions. The goal is to make every page useful even before you run the tool.

Privacy

GlobalTools does not require registration for core tools. The platform may use analytics and advertising services, as explained in the Privacy Policy. Never submit confidential information to a tool unless you are sure the processing method is appropriate for your case.

Advertising and sustainability

GlobalTools may display advertising to help maintain the platform, servers, libraries and development. Advertising is separate from tool functionality and does not change the results produced.

Updates

Tools and explanations may be corrected, improved or expanded over time. When a feature depends on a browser, library or third-party service, its operation may also depend on those components.

Contact us

Found an error, confusing explanation or a tool that could be better? Send a message through the contact page.
